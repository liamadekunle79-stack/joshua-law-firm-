Twelve Areas of Legal Excellence

Joshua's Firms offers comprehensive legal representation across twelve major practice areas. Each service is delivered with the same commitment to thoroughness, strategic thinking, and client-focused advocacy that defines our firm.

Click any service card to learn more about that practice area. To discuss your specific legal needs, contact our team to schedule a confidential consultation.

Our Practice Areas

01

Corporate & Business Law

Comprehensive legal counsel for businesses at every stage — from incorporation and governance to mergers, acquisitions, joint ventures, and commercial transactions.

Our corporate practice advises Nigerian and multinational companies on regulatory compliance, shareholders' agreements, board governance, and complex commercial negotiations. We understand the Nigerian business environment and provide practical, actionable guidance that supports sustainable business growth.

02

Family Law

Sensitive, experienced representation in divorce, custody, matrimonial property, guardianship, adoption, and child welfare proceedings.

Family legal matters are among the most personal and consequential. Our approach combines legal expertise with genuine sensitivity — ensuring that your rights and your children's best interests are protected throughout the process, while minimising unnecessary conflict wherever possible.

03

Criminal Defense

Rigorous, experienced defense for individuals and entities facing criminal investigation or prosecution in Nigerian federal and state courts.

Our criminal defense attorneys bring extensive courtroom experience and an unwavering commitment to protecting every client's constitutional rights. From initial police investigation through trial and appeal, we provide determined, strategic representation at every stage of criminal proceedings.

04

Real Estate Law

End-to-end legal support for property purchases, sales, leases, title investigations, conveyancing, and real estate dispute resolution across Nigeria.

Property transactions in Nigeria require meticulous due diligence. Our real estate practice covers title searches and perfection, survey regularisation, deed preparation, land use approvals, and resolution of boundary and ownership disputes — protecting your investment at every stage.

05

Civil Litigation

Strategic, determined advocacy in civil disputes — from pre-litigation negotiations through trial representation, judgment enforcement, and appeals.

Our litigation team has a strong track record in Nigerian courts at all levels — Magistrate, High Court, Court of Appeal, and the Supreme Court. We pursue resolution through negotiation where appropriate, and litigate decisively when necessary to protect our clients' interests.

06

Employment Law

Expert advisory and dispute resolution for employers and employees on contracts, dismissal, workplace rights, and labour regulatory compliance.

We advise on employment contract drafting, disciplinary procedures, redundancy, wrongful dismissal claims, and regulatory compliance with Nigerian labour law. We represent clients before the National Industrial Court and assist businesses in designing compliant HR frameworks.

07

Immigration Law

Visa applications, work permits, residency status, citizenship, and immigration compliance for individuals and corporate expatriate workforces.

Our immigration practice assists clients with STR/CERPAC applications, combined expatriate residence permits, business visa regularisation, and immigration appeals. We also advise multinational employers on maintaining compliant expatriate workforces in Nigeria.

08

Personal Injury

Compassionate, assertive representation for individuals who have suffered harm due to negligence, accidents, or unlawful conduct.

We pursue compensation for medical expenses, lost income, pain and suffering, and other damages on behalf of injury victims. Our team handles road accident claims, workplace injuries, medical negligence, and product liability cases with thoroughness and determination.

09

Estate Planning

Wills, trusts, probate administration, and succession planning to protect your legacy and ensure your assets are distributed according to your intentions.

We assist clients in preparing legally sound wills, establishing trusts, navigating probate proceedings, and planning for business succession. Our estate planning services help individuals and families protect their wealth, minimise estate disputes, and provide for future generations.

10

Intellectual Property

Trademark registration, copyright protection, patent advice, and IP litigation to safeguard your creative work and commercial brand assets.

We assist businesses and creative professionals in registering and protecting intellectual property with the Nigerian Trademarks Registry and relevant authorities. We also advise on IP licensing, technology transfer agreements, and infringement disputes — helping clients commercialise and defend their intangible assets.

11

Contract Law

Drafting, review, negotiation, and enforcement of commercial and personal contracts across all industries and transaction types.

Sound contracts are the foundation of every successful business relationship. We draft clear, enforceable agreements — from simple service agreements to complex multi-party commercial contracts — and advise clients on contract disputes, termination, and breach of contract remedies.

12

Tax Law

Tax planning, compliance advisory, and dispute resolution for individuals, corporations, and institutions operating within the Nigerian tax framework.

We assist clients in navigating Nigeria's complex tax landscape — including FIRS assessments, company income tax, VAT compliance, and transfer pricing. Our tax attorneys represent clients in objections and appeals before the Tax Appeal Tribunal and federal courts, and advise on tax-efficient structuring for transactions and investments.
