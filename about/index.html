Our Story

A Firm Founded on
Principle & Purpose.

Joshua's Firms was founded with a clear and unwavering purpose: to provide legal services of the highest standard to individuals, businesses, and institutions in Nigeria — with integrity as its cornerstone.

Over more than two decades of practice in Lagos, we have built a reputation for sound legal advice, thoughtful advocacy, and consistent results. Our clients range from private individuals navigating personal legal challenges to multinational corporations managing complex commercial transactions.

What has remained constant throughout our history is our commitment to treating every client's matter — regardless of scale — with the same level of care, preparation, and professionalism.

What Guides Us

Our Philosophy

Four principles underpin every matter we handle, every client we serve, and every decision we make as a firm.

01

Integrity in Every Action

We hold ourselves to the highest ethical standards. Our clients can trust that every piece of advice we offer reflects our honest, professional assessment — never what is merely convenient or commercially expedient.

02

The Client Comes First

Our decisions are always guided by what serves our clients best. We invest time in understanding your circumstances, your objectives, and your concerns — because great legal advice begins with genuine understanding.

03

Excellence in Practice

We are committed to the pursuit of legal excellence. Our attorneys engage in continuous professional development, stay current with legal developments, and approach every engagement with rigorous preparation.

04

Respect & Discretion

We treat every client with the utmost respect and handle sensitive legal matters with absolute discretion. Confidentiality is not merely a professional obligation — it is a fundamental expression of our respect for those we serve.
